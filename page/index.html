01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

More than a game.
A piece of history.

Minions Paradise was a mobile game that let players build and explore a tropical island with the Minions. After the original service ended, its online experience became inaccessible.

Minions Paradise Brazil is an independent fan initiative exploring digital preservation, client compatibility, server architecture and technical research related to the game.

Our goal is to document what we learn, investigate technical possibilities and work toward preserving a piece of mobile gaming history.

05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions from paradise.

Is the game playable right now?+

This website does not announce a playable release. Research and compatibility work are experimental, and a working experience depends on resolving technical challenges.

Is this an official EA project?+

No. Minions Paradise Brazil is an independent fan initiative. It is not affiliated with, authorized, sponsored or endorsed by Electronic Arts, Illumination or Universal.

Why is there no download button?+

No authorized release is announced on this website. We will only publish downloads when there is appropriate material that can responsibly and lawfully be distributed.

Is the project open source?+

The website code can be shared independently. The game's original code, artwork and other proprietary assets are separate and are not automatically open source.
